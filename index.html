About Us

Kerio Valley Conservancy (KVC) is a community-based organization dedicated to addressing poverty, climate vulnerability, and environmental degradation along the hanging Kerio Valley through sustainable, community-led solutions...

Our Vision

To be a leading human and environment centered CBO in the region that provides inclusive, innovative, and sustainable programs and services aimed at improving individuals' and societies' wellbeing.

Our Mission

To protect people and the environment by promoting agroforestry and providing alternative sources of livelihood that foster peace and help halt pollution and climate change.
