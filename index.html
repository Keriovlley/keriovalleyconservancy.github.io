About Us

Kerio Valley Conservancy (KVC) is a community-based organization dedicated to addressing poverty, climate vulnerability, and environmental degradation along the hanging Kerio Valley through sustainable, community-led solutions. Kerio Valley is a vital but fragile landscape in Kenya where people, nature, and livelihoods are closely connected, yet years of deforestation, unsustainable farming, recurrent fires, and climate stress have led to degraded soils, drying rivers, biodiversity loss, and rising poverty. Restoring these landscapes is transforming the valley through community-led agroforestry, reforestation, soil and water conservation, and climate-resilient farming that rebuilds ecosystems while improving household incomes.

Our Vision

To be a leading human and environment centered CBO in the region that provides inclusive, innovative, and sustainable programs and services aimed at improving individuals' and societies' wellbeing.

Our Mission

To protect people and the environment by promoting agroforestry and providing alternative sources of livelihood that foster peace and help halt pollution and climate change.

What We Do

Agroforestry

Promoting tree planting and sustainable land use practices to restore soil health and biodiversity.

Climate-Smart Agriculture

Empowering farmers with techniques to improve yields while adapting to climate change.

Environmental Conservation

Protecting and restoring natural ecosystems and watersheds in the valley.

Sustainable Livelihoods

Creating economic opportunities that are in harmony with environmental stewardship.
