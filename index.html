About Us

Kerio Valley Conservancy (KVC) is a community-based organization dedicated to addressing poverty, climate vulnerability, and environmental degradation along the Kerio Valley through sustainable community-led solutions.

Our Vision

To be a leading human and environment centered CBO providing inclusive and sustainable programs that improve community wellbeing.

Our Mission

To protect people and the environment by promoting agroforestry and alternative livelihoods that help address climate change.

What We Do

Agroforestry

Promoting tree planting and sustainable land use.

Climate-Smart Agriculture

Helping farmers adapt to climate change.

Environmental Conservation

Restoring ecosystems and protecting watersheds.

Sustainable Livelihoods

Creating income opportunities for communities.
